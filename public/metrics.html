Vision Check for Kids — Success Metrics

Lenskart Co-founder Review Edition

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Block 1 — Primary Metric

North Star Metric

Attended under-18 eye tests attributable to Vision Check that resulted in a prescription change, per month.
Attended, not booked — an unattended booking is worth zero. And resulted in a change, because a referral that finds nothing means the signal fired wrongly. Deliberately not app sessions or store walk-ins: both rise when the product gets pushier, not when a child sees better.

Funnel Conversion

Block 3 — Core Outcomes

Outcomes

Prescription freshness
Share of under-18 prescriptions on file under 12 months old. The key long-term metric this product exists to move, measurable directly from order data.
Time to correction
Median days from initial screening session to glasses delivered in the child's hands.
Block 4 — Diagnostic Reliability

Signal Quality

Accuracy is the wrong metric. At ~15% prevalence, classifying every child CLEAR scores 85% accuracy and catches nobody. Precision, recall, and NPV are tracked separately because they cost different amounts to obtain.
Precision (PPV) — Free
TP / (TP + FP)
Of children referred, the share who genuinely needed correction. Ground truth obtained on all walk-ins.
Recall — Budgeted
TP / (TP + FN)
Measured by monthly random sampling of CLEAR children offered a free in-store test.
NPV — Safety Floor
TN / (TN + FN)
The safety floor. Directly ties to the P0 false negative guardrail (1 − NPV).
Ground Truth Standard
"Needed correction" = in-store refraction issued a new prescription or changed existing power by ≥ 0.50 D in either eye.
Test-Retest Protocol
Two sessions 7 days apart with no clinical change expected to establish baseline screening reliability before setting thresholds.
Block 5 — Risk Control & Non-Negotiables

Guardrails & Anti-Metric

Guardrail Trigger Action
False negatives (P0) — CLEAR needing power change in 90d > 10% of CLEAR Pull feature
False positives — REFER finding no power change > 40% of REFER Tighten threshold (prevents commercial drift)
Child-facing leakage — Clinical terms shown to child Any instance P0 defect
Anti-Metric: Sales Attach Rate

Frame and lens attach rate on resulting visit.

Must never appear in team goals or experiment criteria. Optimising it converts a health check into a sales trigger.

Coupled alarm: Audit threshold if attach rate grows faster than attendance for 2 consecutive months.
