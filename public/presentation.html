Product Case Study

Vision Check for Kids (Shape Chase)

A parent checks their child's eyesight at home in 60 seconds. The child thinks he is playing a game. The result reaches the parent securely inside the Lenskart App, never reaching the child.

The Problem & Evidence

A child's eyes change faster than anything in a family's life reports. Nothing in a normal week tells a parent that their child stopped seeing clearly four months ago.

13.1% Myopic & 75.3% Uncorrected The AIIMS Delhi Myopia Study screened 9,884 urban schoolchildren: 13.1% were myopic, and only 24.7% of those were wearing appropriate spectacles. Three in four myopic children were uncorrected or wrongly corrected.
45.2% Parental Correlation A North India study found 45.2% of myopic children had at least one myopic parent. For a myopic parent on file, their child is a pre-qualified target.

Two Invisible Failures:

  • The Undetected Child: Has never seen a blackboard sharply, so nothing feels lost. He copes (front row, photographing the board) and every coping mechanism quietens the signal.
  • The Under-Corrected Child: Already has glasses. Power drifted 7 months ago. He gets replacements when a frame physically breaks, fitted against old prescriptions.
User Personas

Optimizes for concealment. Doesn't want to become the topic at home or lose status in sports. If he wears glasses, he fears a stronger power.

Optimizes for certainty. Screen time absorbs every symptom. Taking a child to an optician costs a half-day.

Job to be done Work out if slipping marks are effort, attention, or vision
Pain Point No trigger she trusts; frame breakage cycle replaces without re-testing
Pediatric Shape Chase Engine

A 60-second PWA game played by the child using pediatric Lea symbols (Heart ❤️, House 🏠, Circle 🟢, Square 🟨, Star ⭐, Triangle 🔺, Moon 🌙, Balloon 🎈).

1. Asymmetric Output Design

The child sees scores, combos, and ranks. Never an acuity score or verdict. Takes away the incentive to conceal.

2. 40 cm Near-Acuity Alignment

Calibrated for hand-held distance (40 cm / 1.3 ft). Child taps screen directly — zero microphone permission or shouting required.

3. Specsy Dino Real-time Distance Guard

Camera feed monitors distance during play. If child leans <35 cm or drifts >50 cm, Dino mascot pauses play with a speech bubble warning.

User Journey & Setup
Step 1 — Parent App Entry

Generic Lenskart Marketing Banner

Parent clicks unpersonalized ad on homepage. Grants DPDP consent and approves 2x1 white home screen widget installation.

Step 2 — Handover & Widget Launch

Child Home Screen Widget

Parent hands phone to child. Child taps the white Shape Chase widget icon on the mobile home screen to start PWA.

Step 3 — Gameplay & Child Calibration

Child Face Alignment & Shape Chase

Child calibrates face alignment on webcam right before starting. Plays 12 shape matching rounds with floating +100 point celebrations.

Step 4 — Result Sync

Secure In-App Dashboard Report

Results sync securely to the parent's Lenskart app (bypassing third-party WhatsApp preview risks). Offers 1-click optometrist booking.

False Negatives Trigger
